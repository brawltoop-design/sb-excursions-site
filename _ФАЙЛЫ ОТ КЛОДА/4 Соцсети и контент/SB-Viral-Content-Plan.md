**Post 9:**
The "I survived Bali traffic" starter pack:

- Grab bike, not Grab car (cuts travel time in half)
- Never trust Google Maps "15 minutes" — it's always 40
- Rent a scooter only if you've ridden before (Bali roads are no joke)
- Hire a driver for day trips (from $35-45/day, way less stress)
- Avoid Kuta-Seminyak corridor 4-7pm
- School drop-off time = chaos everywhere

You WILL sit in traffic. Accept it. Plan around it.
